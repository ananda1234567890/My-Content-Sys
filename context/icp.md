## Primary ICP: The Growth-Hungry YouTuber

**Who:** YouTube creators with 10K–500K subscribers who are serious about growing but hitting a retention ceiling
**Industry:** Any niche — their problem is platform-specific, not niche-specific
**Location:** English-speaking markets (US, UK, Canada, Australia) — they pay in USD
**Pain:** Their videos look fine but retention drops off, watch time is low, views are inconsistent
**Goal:** Break through to the next subscriber milestone; get into YouTube's recommendation engine
**Budget:** $500–$3000/month for a dedicated editor
**Decision trigger:** They see a video from a competitor that's blowing up and realize the edit is doing the heavy lifting

**What they respond to on LinkedIn:**
- Specific retention techniques they haven't tried
- Breakdowns of why viral videos work (editing angle)
- Before/after edit comparisons
- YouTube algorithm insights from an editor's POV
- AI tools that speed up editing without sacrificing quality

## Secondary ICP: The Ambitious New Creator

**Who:** Creators with <10K subscribers who are still figuring out their workflow
**Pain:** Spending too much time editing, not sure what makes a "good" edit for their niche
**Goal:** Learn faster, stop reinventing the wheel
**Budget:** Lower — may not hire yet, but follows and eventually becomes a client or referral

**What they respond to:**
- Step-by-step technique breakdowns
- Tool recommendations with honest takes
- "I wish someone told me this earlier" style posts
