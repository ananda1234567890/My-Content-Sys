# Premiere Pro's Hidden Fix for Warped Sound Effects

## Post Text (copy-paste ready)

Every time you stretch a sound effect to fit your edit, Premiere is warping the pitch along with it.

Wait, what did I just say?
Yup. Rate-stretch a riser, shorten a whoosh, slow a hit effect — and that clean audio turns into a crushed, robotic mess.

→ SFX is too long for the cut
→ You drag it shorter
→ Now it sounds wrong
→ So you either leave it or cut it entirely

Here's the thing. Premiere has had a fix for this for years and most editors never find it.

It's a checkbox. ONE checkbox.

Do this:
→ Select the audio clip on your timeline
→ Press Ctrl + R (Cmd + R on Mac) to open Clip Speed/Duration
→ Check the box that says "Maintain Audio Pitch"
→ Now adjust the speed or duration however you need

That's it. The timing changes, the pitch stays locked.

Your risers still sound like risers after you've stretched them to fit.
Your SFX snap to cut points without sounding like a cartoon.

P.S. This works on music clips too. If you ever trim a track's tail and it goes weird, same checkbox :)
