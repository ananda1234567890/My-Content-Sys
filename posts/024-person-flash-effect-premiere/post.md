# Person Flash Effect in Premiere Pro

## Post Text (copy-paste ready)

Talking-head edits that look expensive usually have one thing in common.

They're using visual micro-moments to break up the monotony. Things you feel more than consciously notice.

Here's one you can build in Premiere Pro in about 5 minutes — a "person flash" effect. No plugins, no third-party packs. Just built-in tools used in a specific order.

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟭: Cut out your moment
Find the exact point in your talking-head clip where you want the flash to hit. Cut it out as its own segment on the timeline.

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟮: Duplicate the clip onto the track above
Copy the segment and paste it directly above the original. Two identical clips, stacked.

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟯: Mask the subject on the top layer
Select the top clip. In Effect Controls, use the Object Mask tool to isolate just the person in frame. The background cuts out of this layer entirely.

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟰: Add three cuts at the start of the masked layer
Make two cuts very close together at the beginning of the top clip to create three short segments.
→ First segment (will flash white)
→ Second segment (normal)
→ Third segment (will flash white)
Now you've got the three-beat structure.

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟱: Create the white-out with Lumetri Color
On the first and third segments, add Lumetri Color. Open the Curves panel and drag the line all the way to the top. Full white. That's the flash.

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟲: Sell it with sound
Place a sharp flash SFX or whoosh at the cut points. Audio is what makes this feel like a deliberate beat rather than a glitch.

The technique has been inside Premiere this whole time.
You're just not combining the tools in this order.

Try it.
