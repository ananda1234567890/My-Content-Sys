# The Breaking Bad Problem in Creator Content

**Date created:** 2026-06-13
**Source:** @hey.deshna
**Format:** AI Infographic
**Platform:** LinkedIn
**Status:** Draft

## Post Text (copy-paste ready)

Most content fails not because the information is wrong
But because there's no one to root for

Wait, what?

→ You watch Ali Abdaal. You're not just learning productivity. You're following someone building a life that actually works
→ You watch MrBeast. You're not just watching challenges. You're watching someone push further every single time
→ You follow a creator for months. You're actually following a character

This is the Breaking Bad problem. And almost every creator skips it.

Walter White isn't compelling because he makes meth. He's compelling because you watch him decide to make meth, then go further, then further, then lose everything. The ARC is the show.

Creator content works the same way.

Here's the thing. Viewers don't follow experts. They follow protagonists.

Do this:
→ Stop writing content as a list of things you know. Start writing it as a character going through something
→ Your "character" has a clear starting point, a specific tension or challenge, and a direction they're moving in. The audience wants to see if you get there
→ You don't need to manufacture drama. The tension is already built in. "I'm trying to build X while also dealing with Y." That's a character. That's someone to follow

That's it.

The difference between a creator with 500 followers and 500,000 usually isn't information quality.

It's whether there's a protagonist.

P.S. The creators you genuinely look forward to seeing post? Every single one of them has one :)
