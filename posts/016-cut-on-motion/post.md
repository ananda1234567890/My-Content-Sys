## Post Text (copy-paste ready)

Why do your cuts feel choppy?

You're landing them on still frames.

Wait, back up.

Yup. The edit lives or dies based on WHERE in the motion you make the cut.

When the subject is completely still and you cut, the viewer's brain reads it as a disruption. There's no movement to hide the jump. It just feels jarring.

But cut on the middle of a natural movement, and the eye follows the motion right through the transition. The edit disappears.

The best editors in the world make every cut land on:
→ a head starting to turn
→ a hand mid-gesture
→ a shoulder just beginning to shift
→ any natural moment of motion in the frame

That's the actual technique. It takes like 5 seconds per cut.

So how do you fix it?

Here's how to build smooth edits from ONE habit change.

Do this:
→ Scrub your footage and hunt for natural motion moments before placing any cut
→ Drop the cut right as the movement begins, not before and not after
→ Do this consistently for every cut in the sequence
→ After a while it becomes instinct

That's it. No plugins, no tricks.

Your cuts will feel pro-level before you touch anything else in the edit.

P.S. Works on interviews, talking heads, vlogs, any footage with a subject in it. Once you start looking for motion moments, you'll see them everywhere :)
