## Post Text (copy-paste ready)

Higgsfield can bridge two completely separate shots with a single AI clip and I feel like nobody is ACTUALLY doing this.

Here's how to make any cut invisible using Kling 3.0:

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟭: Shoot your two clips
You need two separate shots — the clip before the cut and the clip after. They don't need to match in framing or angle.
→ Clip 1: ends on the frame right before the action (product on a table, hand near an object)
→ Clip 2: starts at the connecting moment (the grab, the reach, the transition action)

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟮: Capture your bridge frames
Pause clip 1 on the exact last frame before the cut. Screenshot it. Then pause clip 2 on the first frame after the cut. Screenshot that one too. These two images are your start frame and end frame.

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟯: Open Higgsfield and select Kling 3.0
In the image-to-video section, choose Kling 3.0 as your model. Upload your first screenshot as the "start frame" and the second as the "end frame."

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟰: Write the motion prompt
Describe the movement that connects the two frames — the hand sliding forward, the object being picked up, whatever bridges them physically.
→ Keep it specific and physical: describe what moves and in which direction
→ Generate and pick the version that flows cleanest

Now you've got an AI clip that naturally animates from one shot to the next.

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟱: Assemble in your editor
Drop clip 1, then the AI bridge clip, then clip 2. Trim to the exact frames you want.

That's a match cut that looks shot on set, from two clips that were never in the same frame.
