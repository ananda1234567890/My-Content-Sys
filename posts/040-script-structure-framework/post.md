# Why Your Client's Edit Underperforms (It's the Script)

**Date created:** 2026-06-13
**Source:** @hey.deshna
**Format:** Carousel

## Post Text (copy-paste ready)

Your edit is performing fine. Your client's script is losing them viewers before a single cut matters.

That will actually save you hours of trying to fix a structural problem with a visual solution.

Here's how to audit and rewrite any script using the Relatable Authority before Action framework:

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟭: Identify the Generic Hook
Read the first 3 lines of the script out loud
→ Does it open with what the product or service does?
→ Does it start with a benefit claim?
→ Does it assume the viewer already cares?

If yes to any of these, the hook needs rebuilding. A viewer who doesn't already trust the brand will scroll past every one of those openers.

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟮: Rewrite With a Pattern Interrupt
Replace the generic hook by challenging a belief the audience already holds
→ "85% of people dealing with acne have been treating it in the wrong order."
→ That's a pattern interrupt. It stops the scroll by making the viewer question something they thought they already knew
Don't open with the product. Open with the audience's wrong assumption.

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟯: Add the Credibility Layer
After the interrupt, earn the right to keep talking
→ Drop one real stat or a specific piece of research
→ Keep it short: one sentence, one number, one fact
→ This is what makes the viewer feel understood rather than sold to

𝗦𝘁𝗲𝗽 𝟰: Deliver the Solution in Ingredient Language
Don't describe what the product does. Describe how it works
→ Not: "Our formula clears acne"
→ Yes: "This combines niacinamide and zinc to block the enzyme that triggers oil overproduction"

Viewers trust specificity. Generic benefits feel like ads. Specific mechanisms feel like education.

The edit is the last 20% of the job. The script is the first 80%.

Learn to see both and clients will keep you around a lot longer.
