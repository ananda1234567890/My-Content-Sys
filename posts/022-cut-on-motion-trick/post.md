## Post Text (copy-paste ready)

Why do your cuts feel choppy even when the pacing is technically right?

Because you're cutting on stillness.

Wait, what did I just say?
Yup. Most editors cut when the subject finishes a sentence, takes a breath, or pauses.

→ You find the stillness
→ You make the cut
→ The timeline looks clean
→ And the edit still feels mechanical somehow

Here's the thing. Stillness is the worst moment to cut.

The eye has nothing to follow through the edit, so the jump becomes visible.

So how do you fix it?
Cut on MOTION instead.

Do this:
→ Watch for when the subject starts a gesture, head shift, or body lean
→ Make your cut at the very beginning of that movement
→ The movement continues across the cut without the brain registering a break

That's it. The motion acts as a visual bridge and the cut disappears.

Your edit feels snappier without being faster and more dynamic without being louder.

P.S. Start with hand gestures. They're the most consistent movement in talking-head footage and the easiest to find on the timeline :)
