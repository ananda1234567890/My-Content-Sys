# Why Founder Content Fails (And How to Fix It)

**Date created:** 2026-06-13
**Source:** @hey.deshna
**Format:** AI Infographic
**Platform:** LinkedIn
**Status:** Draft

## Post Text (copy-paste ready)

Most creator content isn't bad because it lacks value
It's bad because it lacks a person

Yup. THAT's the actual problem.

→ Tips without context feel like a Wikipedia article
→ Advice without stakes feels like a press release
→ Expertise without vulnerability feels like a lecture

And all of it gets scrolled past. Fast.

Here's the thing. Viewers don't follow topic experts. They follow characters who happen to be experts.

The difference is simple.

Information-driven content: "Here are 5 ways to grow your audience."
Character-driven content: "I had 400 followers for 18 months and couldn't figure out why nothing was working. This is what I finally changed."

Same information. Completely different relationship to the viewer.

One of them has someone to root for. The other one is just content.

Do this:
→ Before you write your next post or video script, ask: who is the character in this? What do they want? What's in the way?
→ You don't have to manufacture conflict. The honest tension is already there. Share the thing you're still trying to figure out, not just the thing you already solved
→ Give the viewer a reason to follow, not just a reason to save the post

The save is nice. The follow means they want to see what happens next.

That's the difference between a content library and a creator people actually care about.

P.S. The creators you genuinely look forward to? Every single one of them has a character :)
