# The 3-Second Retention Rule

**Date created:** 2026-06-13
**Source:** @socialcontentking
**Format:** AI Infographic
**Platform:** LinkedIn
**Status:** Draft

## Post Text (copy-paste ready)

Your content isn't losing viewers because the topic is boring
It's losing them because nothing is changing on screen

Yup. That's it.

→ Same framing for 8 seconds? Drop-off
→ Same camera angle for the whole video? Drop-off
→ Same visual density with no breaks in between? Drop-off

The 3-second rule is simple: change something on screen every 3 seconds.

Not a full scene change. Not a new camera setup. Just SOMETHING.

A cut. A zoom. A text overlay appearing or disappearing. A graphic. A b-roll clip. A reaction shot. Anything that visually resets the viewer's attention before it has a chance to drift.

Here's how to apply it to your existing content:
→ Watch your last video and count how many seconds pass between visual changes
→ Anywhere you go past 5 to 6 seconds with a static shot, that's a drop-off point
→ Add a cut, a text element, or a shot change in those gaps

Rinse and repeat.

This isn't about making your edit feel choppy. Done right, it feels dynamic and intentional. The videos hitting the highest retention rates aren't the most cinematic ones, they're the ones that never give the viewer's thumb a reason to swipe.

3 seconds. ONE visual change. That's the whole rule.

Save this.
