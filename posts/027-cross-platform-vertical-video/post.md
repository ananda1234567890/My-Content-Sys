## Post Text (copy-paste ready)

You spent 4 hours on that vertical video and posted it to one platform.

After tracking content performance across multiple platforms.
And watching the same 60-second clip pull very different numbers depending on where it landed.
I've seen editors leave most of their reach on the table.
Not because the content was wrong.
Because they stopped after one upload.

Many believe reposting to other platforms feels lazy or spammy...
The truth?
Every short-form platform is running a different audience with almost zero overlap between them.
Here's what that means in practice:

The Platforms
YouTube Shorts crossed 70 billion daily views in 2024, and search-based discovery means your clip can surface weeks later. Instagram Reels drives more engagement than feed posts and Stories combined. TikTok remains the primary discovery engine for new audiences. LinkedIn is rewarding vertical video with significantly higher organic reach than text posts right now. X is still early on vertical video, which means less competition for eyeballs.

One file. Five distribution channels. Almost no audience overlap.

What to Change Per Platform
The video file can be the same. The packaging should differ.
1. TikTok: hook in the first half-second, trend-aware audio if relevant
2. Instagram: clean cover frame, polished visuals
3. YouTube Shorts: searchable title, keywords in the description
4. LinkedIn: longer and more detailed caption than the other platforms
5. X: post the video with a written thread above it for extra reach

The One Rule That Kills Cross-Posting
Remove any platform watermarks before reposting. Instagram actively suppresses content with a TikTok logo. YouTube de-prioritizes Reels watermarks. Export clean, post everywhere (and this is the most important thing on the list, tbh).

You already made the content. The distribution is just posting five more times.
And you'll reach more people from one video than most editors do in a week.
