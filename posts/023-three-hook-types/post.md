## Post Text (copy-paste ready)

Most editors are fixing their pacing, their color, their transitions.

And their hooks are still losing viewers in 2 seconds flat.

After studying what actually works in short-form video.
And auditing reels with solid edits but bad retention.
I kept seeing the same pattern.
The edit was solid. The hook was doing nothing.
I've figured out there are 3 specific types of hooks and most people only use one.

Many believe a good verbal opener is all you need...
The truth?
The hook is not just what you say. It's three things happening at once.
Here's the framework:

Verbal Hook
This is what comes out of your mouth in the first second. Say something that forces a response or creates an open loop: a question, a bold claim, or a "wait, what?" statement. The goal is to make the brain feel like it would be losing something by scrolling away.

Visual Hook
This is what the eye sees before the brain processes the words. Fast-paced B-roll, a jarring visual setup, an unexpected object in frame. The visual hook works in parallel with the verbal hook, not after it.

Text Hook
This is the overlay or caption that gives the viewer a reason to keep watching before the video even starts. One line, high contrast, positioned so it's the first thing read. "How I went from X to Y." "This took me 3 months to figure out." "Stop doing this."

All three can run simultaneously in a single first frame (and the reels that hold attention consistently are almost always doing all three at once).

You're not building one hook. You're building three locks on the same door.
And you'll stop losing viewers in the first second faster than you'd expect.
